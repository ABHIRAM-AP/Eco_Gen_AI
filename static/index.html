EcoGen-AI Mathematical Framework & Platform Architecture
Scientific references: Patterson et al. (2021), GHG Protocol Scope 2/3, Lacoste et al. (2019)

System Architecture Overview

Layer 1: Ingestion & Hardware Telemetry

• Intercepts LLM inference invocations via Python decorators or REST endpoints.
• Captures runtime wall-clock duration, input/output tokens, and power draw.
• Pulls real-time grid carbon intensity from WattTime API and UK National Grid API.
• Periodically samples idle system power to establish baseline power (P_idle).

Layer 2: Analytical Engine & Benchmarking

• Implements 2-Tier Energy Accounting: Tier 1 measured power with idle baseline subtraction; Tier 2 theoretical FLOPs fallback.
• GHG Scope 2 (Operational Grid Carbon) and Scope 3 (Embodied Hardware Amortization).
• Task benchmarking across text, image, code assistance, and fine-tuning.
• What-If optimization simulator for cloud/datacenter hardware and grid planning.

Guide Review Q&A Highlights

Q: How do you measure when power telemetry is missing?
A: We gracefully fall back to Tier 2: FLOPs ≈ 2 × ActiveParams × Tokens against GPU peak theoretical TFLOPS and TDP.

Q: Why subtract idle power?
A: To avoid penalizing the AI model for background OS tasks, measuring only incremental marginal energy.

Mathematical Formulations

Tier 1: Measured Energy (kWh) with PUE & Idle Subtraction
E = ((P_active - P_idle) × runtime_sec × PUE × num_gpus) / 3,600,000
Where P is in Watts, runtime in seconds, and 3,600,000 is Ws per kWh.
Tier 2: FLOPs-Based Energy Estimation (Patterson et al. 2021)
FLOPs ≈ 2 × active_params × 10⁹ × total_tokens E ≈ (FLOPs / (peak_TFLOPS × 10¹²)) × TDP_W × PUE × num_gpus / 3,600,000
Applied with an empirical hardware utilization coefficient (η ≈ 0.45).
Scope 2: Operational Carbon Footprint
Scope2_gCO₂eq = Energy_kWh × Grid_Carbon_Intensity_(gCO₂eq/kWh)
Regional carbon intensity obtained dynamically from live grid APIs.
Scope 3: Embodied Manufacturing Carbon Amortization
Embodied_gCO₂eq = (GPU_Manufacturing_gCO₂eq / Lifespan_sec) × runtime_sec
Amortizes silicon manufacturing and server lifecycle over 3-year expected lifetime.

EcoGen-AI Guide Review Walkthrough & Demo Script

5-Minute Presentation Flow for Tomorrow

  1. 1. Problem Statement & Motivation: Generative AI inference consumes staggering amounts of electricity, yet developers lack standardized, real-time carbon auditing.
  2. 2. Layer 1 Ingestion: Show the "Layer 1: Ingestion Feed" tab. Demonstrate how inference duration, input/output tokens, and power telemetry are tracked across local edge models (Ollama) and cloud APIs (Gemini).
  3. 3. Live Event Ingestion: Click "Log Event", choose Gemma-3 or Gemini Flash, and click submit. Show that the backend computes energy and carbon immediately and updates all metrics.
  4. 4. Layer 2 Analytical Engine: Show the "Executive Dashboard" and "Benchmarks" tabs. Highlight the 2-tier energy computation (measured vs FLOPs fallback) and Scope 2 + Scope 3 embodied carbon.
  5. 5. What-If Optimization Sandbox: Switch to the "What-If Simulator" tab. Show how migrating a model from a coal grid to France (nuclear/clean) or modernizing from PUE 1.6 to 1.1 delivers up to 70%+ carbon reductions.
